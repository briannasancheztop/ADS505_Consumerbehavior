# ADS505_Consumerbehavior

Short Description of Your Project and Objectives: 
This project will be analyzing the shift from in-store to online purchasing with the key area focus being demographic, loyalty, and purchasing data. We will identify drivers of channel preference, repeat purchases, and spending levels. Project objective is to provide actionable insights, strengthen loyalty, improve omnichannel strategy and optimize physical store presence.

Name of Your Selected Dataset and Programming Language: 
Dataset: shopping_behavior_updated.csv
Programming Language: Python
 
Description of Your Selected Dataset (source, number of variables, size of the dataset, etc.): 
Dataset comes from Kaggle
This data set has 3900 rows records and 18 columns.
Column names: 'Customer ID', 'Age', 'Gender', 'Item Purchased', 'Category', 'Purchase Amount (USD)', 'Location', 'Size', 'Color', 'Season', 'Review Rating', 'Subscription Status', 'Shipping Type', 'Discount Applied', 'Promo Code Used', 'Previous Purchases', 'Payment Method', 'Frequency of Purchases'
Team Project Business Brief
 
Purpose:
Retailers are experiencing declining foot traffic as customers are shifting more into online shopping. This shift threatens profitability and long term viability. To remain competitive, the company must adapt to evolving consumer preferences by integrating stronger omnichannel strategies.

Background:
Department stores have historically relied on in-store foot traffic to generate sales, using promotions, marketing, and customer service as key drivers of revenue. While this model worked in the past, the rise of e-commerce and evolving consumer expectations have disrupted traditional approaches. As online shopping becomes more dominant, retailers must rethink their strategies to balance physical store experiences with digital convenience.
 
Current Situation:
Analysis of consumer behavior data reveals clear differences in demographics and channel preferences. Younger consumers tend to prefer online shopping, while older customers are more inclined toward in-store purchases. Product preferences also vary by channel: online purchases lean toward Other categories (likely electronics), whereas in-store purchases are concentrated in clothing. Review ratings and loyalty status strongly correlate with repeat purchases and higher spending, making them valuable predictors of long-term customer value.
 
Expected Conclusion:
We recommend the following strategic actions:
Invest in Loyalty & Retention – Use subscription/loyalty data and review ratings to identify and retain top-value customers with personalized offers.
Optimize Store Footprint & Experiences – Reposition stores as experiential hubs (styling, events, services) while scaling back underperforming locations to cut unnecessary costs.
Strengthen Omnichannel Integration – Ensure consistency in promotions, pricing, and customer experiences across online and offline channels to build trust and convenience.
By aligning our strategy with the insights from consumer behavior data, the company can capture growth online, strengthen physical stores, and build stronger customer loyalty.
